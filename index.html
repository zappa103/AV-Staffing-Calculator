The math: Estimated weighted BRE = (Active Learning rooms × 3.5) + (all other rooms × 1.9). Those multipliers come from the 2026 portfolio's own average BRE weight for each group, held constant across scenarios so growth is measured in real terms rather than a shifting yardstick — recalibrate them if the underlying rubric changes materially.

Accuracy: at the campus-wide level this reproduces the full room-by-room audit almost exactly. At the individual-area level, expect it to run roughly 5–12% off in either direction — close enough for a screening conversation, not a substitute for the full model when a specific staffing decision is on the table.

Scenarios you save privately stay in this browser only. Scenarios you check "Share with everyone" on are written to a shared Google Sheet and visible to anyone who opens this page — including the room numbers and whatever name you give it, so don't put anything in a shared scenario you wouldn't want a colleague to see.
